# Operating rules

Channel: Hinglish AI tools + AI-assisted freelancing for Indian students/freelancers. Faceless, AI-cinematic visuals, bold fast tone. See `strategy/PLAN.md`.

## Workflow (every video)
1. Research with web search first (trends, tool changes, competitor Shorts). Log sources in `output/research/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
2. Script via the `script` skill into `scripts/`. Hook in first 2 seconds, one idea, ends with CTA to the pinned link.
3. Visuals/voice via Higgsfield (video, image, audio MCP). Thumbnails/graphics via Adobe MCP. Burn Hinglish captions.
4. Queue for human approval of the final cut, then schedule via Metricool. The human clicks publish/approves.
5. After 24h, pull analytics and write `output/analytics/` notes; feed winners back into the next scripts.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable, protects the channel)
- Every video must be genuinely different and human-directed: unique script, angle and demo. No templated slideshow loops, no reposted clips ("inauthentic content" gets channels demonetized or terminated).
- No income guarantees, no "earn ₹X/day" claims, no fake screenshots or testimonials. Show real tool demos and real results only.
- No finance/health/legal advice from an AI persona.
- Disclose AI-generated realistic visuals/voice using YouTube's altered-content toggle.
- Only use licensed or generated music, images and clips. No copyrighted footage.
- Affiliate links must be disclosed.
- Never spend money or enter payment details without explicit human approval.
