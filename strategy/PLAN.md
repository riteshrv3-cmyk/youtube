# Plan

## Niche (decision)
**Hinglish AI tools + AI-powered freelancing/side-skills** ("AI se kaam asaan, skill se kamai").
Why: research points to AI-tools content as one of the fastest-growing niches ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/profitable-ai-niches-youtube/)), Shorts reward single-idea 30s tool demos, and Indian creator guides rank AI tools/tech second after finance ([Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/fastest-growing-youtube-niches-india-2026), single, unverified source). Finance is avoided: higher RPM but AI-persona finance advice is non-monetizable and risky. Hindi-specific growth benchmarks were not found; validate with the first 5 videos.

## Offer ladder (the actual money)
| Tier | Product | Price | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet | Free "10 AI Prompts" PDF | free | email/WhatsApp capture |
| Core | **AI Freelancer Starter Kit (Hinglish)**: 100 prompts, 5 step-by-step workflows, proposal and invoice templates | ₹499 | instant download, UPI |
| Upsell | **Done-for-you AI Shorts pack** for small businesses: 10 branded Shorts | ₹7,999 | 5-day delivery |
| Affiliate | Tools shown in videos (disclosed) | variable | pinned comment |

Payments: Razorpay/Cashfree on own site, or a creator platform (SuperProfile/Topmate/Gumroad with UPI). Pick one in week 1; the human creates the merchant account (KYC).

## Revenue math (honest)
Target ₹85,000. One workable mix: 40 kits (₹20k) + 6 service packs (₹48k) + affiliate (₹17k). From zero followers in 15 days this is a stretch: the service upsell (direct outreach to local businesses with your Shorts as proof) is the fastest lever. Treat 15 days as the first sprint; the funnel keeps compounding after.

## Pipeline
Trend research → script → AI visuals + Hinglish voice → captions → approve → Metricool schedule → analytics → iterate.
Cadence: 2 Shorts/day + 1 long video (6–8 min) every 3 days that expands the best Short.
