---
name: funnel
description: Build and run the offer funnel and outreach for the Starter Kit and done-for-you service.
---

- Keep `site/index.html` current: headline, who it is for, what is inside, price, FAQ, payment link, disclaimer (no income guarantees).
- Draft (never auto-send) outreach messages to local businesses in `output/outreach/`: 3 lines, one sample Short as proof, one clear offer. The human sends or approves sending.
- Draft email/WhatsApp follow-ups for lead-magnet signups. Real testimonials only, with permission.
- Track leads, replies and sales in `output/funnel.csv`.
